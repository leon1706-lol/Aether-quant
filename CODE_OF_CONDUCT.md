# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is a research codebase worked on by people with very different
backgrounds. Participate in good faith: be respectful, be direct without
being hostile, and assume competence in others. Harassment, personal
attacks, and demeaning conduct are not acceptable anywhere in this
project's spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Critique code, data, and claims — not people.
- Keep discussions technical; this repo's tone is direct and evidence-first.
- Accept that backtest results, signal quality, and design choices get
  challenged, sometimes bluntly. Disagreement about the work is normal and
  welcome.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without consent.
- Sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Sustained disruption of discussions after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This policy applies within the repository (issues, PRs, code review,
commit messages) and in all project community spaces: the GitHub
organization, discussions, chat channels, and any event or space claiming
to represent the project. It also applies when representing the project
externally.

## Enforcement

Maintainers enforce this policy and may edit or remove comments, commits,
code, issues, and PRs that violate it, up to and including temporarily or
permanently banning a contributor.

Report violations privately to the maintainers via
[GitHub report/flag mechanisms](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/moderating-comments-and-conversations/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or a direct message to a maintainer. Do not escalate harassment in public
threads.

Reports are handled confidentially to the extent possible; information is
shared only with maintainers who need it to investigate and act. Retaliation
against anyone reporting in good faith is itself a violation.

Maintainers respond on a best-effort basis and will state an outcome to the
reporter where appropriate. Repeated or severe violations lead to permanent
removal from all project spaces.
