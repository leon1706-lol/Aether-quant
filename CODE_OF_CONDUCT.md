# Code of Conduct

Take part in good faith: be respectful, be direct without being hostile, and assume the other person is competent.

It applies in the repository, in every project space, and wherever you represent the project.

## Expected

- Critique code, data and claims, not people.
- Keep discussions technical and lead with evidence.
- Expect your results and designs to be challenged. Disagreement about the work is welcome.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling or intimidation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Carrying on a disruption after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Report privately, not in a public issue:

- **On GitHub:** the "Report content" option on the comment or profile ([how it works](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/moderating-comments-and-conversations/reporting-abuse-or-spam)).
- **To the maintainer:** the contact listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile.

Expect a reply within about a week. Reports stay confidential where possible, and retaliating against a reporter is itself a violation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove a contribution. For a person, the steps are:

1. A private warning that names the behaviour.
2. A temporary ban from the project's spaces.
3. A permanent ban.

A serious case can skip steps.
