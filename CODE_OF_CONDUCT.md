# Code of Conduct

Participate in good faith: be respectful, be direct without being hostile, and assume competence in others.

**Expected**

- Critique code, data and claims, not people.
- Keep discussions technical and evidence-first.
- Expect results and designs to be challenged. Disagreement about the work is welcome.

**Not acceptable**

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling or intimidation.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Sustained disruption after being asked to stop.

**Scope:** the repository and every project space, and when representing the project elsewhere.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may edit or remove contributions and ban contributors, temporarily or permanently. Report privately through [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/moderating-comments-and-conversations/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or a direct message to a maintainer. Reports stay confidential where possible, and retaliation is itself a violation.
